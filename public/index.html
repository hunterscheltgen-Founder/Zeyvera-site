THE APPROACH

Start small. Solve the right problem.

The first objective is not to sell you more software. It is to understand where time, reliability, and handoffs are being lost.

01 / DISCOVER

Describe the current workflow

Capture the process, the people involved, the tools used, and where work slows down or gets repeated.

02 / ASSESS

Identify the friction

Surface manual bottlenecks, single points of failure, missing documentation, and sensible automation candidates.

03 / PRIORITIZE

Leave with next steps

Organize improvements by expected value, effort, dependencies, and risk—not by hype.
