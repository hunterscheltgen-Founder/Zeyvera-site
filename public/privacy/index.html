Information you submit

When you submit the diagnostic form, Zeyvera receives the contact and business information you provide, including descriptions of operational problems. Please do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential customer records, or other sensitive information.

How information is used

Information is used to review your request, assess whether the service may fit your needs, and contact you about that request. It is not sold. The form may be processed by infrastructure and email providers used to operate the website.

Retention and requests

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. To ask about access, correction, or deletion, contact contact@zeyvera.ca.

Security and limitations

Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share.

Updates and legal review

This notice is a basic operational template, not legal advice. Review it for the actual business, vendors, retention practices, and applicable privacy laws before relying on it as a final legal policy.
